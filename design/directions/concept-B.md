## 0. THE CONCEPT

### 0.1 Name
**SOLAR MANIFESTO** — working object name: **THE DISC**.

### 0.2 The big idea (one paragraph)
The site is not a website with a story on it; it is a **kinetic sculpture and a printed manifesto sharing one page**. The sculpture is THE DISC: a sun built from 12,000 hinged gold tesserae on eleven concentric rings (eleven for the eleventh edition), a flip-dot display cast in Byzantine gold — a machine that can *spell*. Every tile flips between a gold face and an ink back, so the Disc can write the word SUN, show the cattle of Helios, break like Burri's *Cretto*, scatter into Miró's stars, re-form as the numeral 11, become a compass, count to infinity, knot into a net, open into a hand, lie down as the horizon, and rise again — one object, fourteen states, no new asset per chapter. Around it the page is a **manifesto set in daylight**: Malta's limestone as the ground, black expanded grotesk for the eleven Articles of the Forum ("LEAVE MORE THAN WE TAKE"), condensed lists for the programme, and the gala's myth demoted — deliberately — to italic marginalia and sharp geometric glyphs in the margin, the way an illuminated manuscript keeps its story in the border. Mediterranean modernism supplies the grammar: Italian Futurism's *parole in libertà* for the type, Matisse's Nice cut-outs for the island, Fontana's slashed canvas for the veil that tears and the net that is cut, Burri's cracked *Cretto* for the shipwreck, Alviani's vibrating aluminium and Le Parc's light for the Disc, Emvin Cremona's Maltese mosaic modernism for the eleven-piece floor, Rothko for the dawn. It is audacious because it is *bright*: while every "cinematic" conference site of 2026 hides in a dark room with a glass torus, this one stands in Mediterranean noon with a gold machine and a black sentence.

### 0.3 The one sentence a visitor should feel
*"Someone built a sun out of gold, wrote a manifesto under it, and the whole thing is asking me — politely, in capitals — what I intend to leave behind."*

### 0.4 What this concept refuses
- No dark-stage-by-default. Limestone daylight is the ground; darkness is an *event* (the storm, the sky of stars, Homer's black).
- No folk-art figures with bodies. Myth is glyph: circle, bar, wedge, arc. No faces, no cloaks, no cattle with legs — a cow is a rectangle with two horns.
- No stacked sections, no bento, no glass, no neon, no marquee, no % counter, no bounce.
- No gold text on paper (1.94:1 — fails). Gold is an *object*, never a word.
- The myth never leads. The Forum leads; the myth annotates. (Concept A's inversion — the client gets a real choice.)

## 1. THE MODERN-ART REGISTER (composition language, never reproduction)

The client asked for "modern art included as part of how the story goes". In this concept each chapter borrows one *Mediterranean* modern or kinetic grammar, used as a rule of composition. All are dead artists or movements cited as influence; nothing is copied.

| Ch | Article | Grammar borrowed | What it gives us |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | Cover | **Olafur Eliasson** *The Weather Project* · **Julio Le Parc** *Continuel-lumière* · **Getulio Alviani** vibrating aluminium surfaces | The sun as an indoor object you stand under; a surface that shimmers because thousands of small facets face slightly different ways |
| I | Stewardship | **Alberto Burri** *Grande Cretto* (Gibellina) · Futurist "lines of force" (Boccioni) | The page cracks along a Voronoi cretto; long diagonals |
| II | Why now | **Joan Miró** *Constellations* | Stars as dots, lines and a few hard shapes; the sky as a diagram |
| III | Ogygia · Who is MTF | **Henri Matisse** cut-outs (*Polynesia, the Sea*; *The Snail*) · **Emvin Cremona** (Maltese modernist) | Flat, scissor-edged planes of sea/limestone/red earth; a Maltese modernism the client can own |
| IV | Unity | **Alexander Calder** mobiles · **Sol LeWitt** wall-drawing instructions | Two hands hanging in balance; connection drawn as rule-based lines |
| V | Three days | **Sonia Delaunay** rhythmic discs · **Bruno Munari** *Macchine inutili* | Three suns as concentric colour; a useless, beautiful mechanism that turns |
| VI | 11 for 11 | **Fortunato Depero** grids and lettering · Roman *opus sectile* (Domvs Romana, Rabat) | Eleven cut stone pieces on a Futurist board |
| VII | The Four | **Giacomo Balla** *Iridescent Interpenetrations* · **Bridget Riley** | A compass of interpenetrating wedges; wave-lines as current |
| VIII | Net Positive | **Roman Opałka** *1965 / 1 – ∞* · **Lucio Fontana** *Concetto spaziale* (the cut) | A number that never stops; a single slash that lets depth in |
| IX | What remains | **Giuseppe Penone** trees · **Agnes Martin** grids | Growth drawn as rule; a faint pencil grid for order |
| X | The Open Hand | **Lucio Fontana** (again — the second cut) · **Chiharu Shiota** thread · Maltese *bizzilla* lace | The net as a wound in the page; the hand as a mosaic |
| XI | I am Homer | **Ad Reinhardt** black paintings | Real black; a single point of light |
| XII | The Application | **El Anatsui** assembled panels · Burri's *sacchi* | A raft assembled from wreckage; a form that is a construction |
| XIII | Colophon | **Mark Rothko** · Delaunay's dawn discs | Colour field to sunrise; the Disc rises for the second time |

The **Futurist** thread runs through all of it: Marinetti's *parole in libertà* (words set free — our width-axis type), Depero's *Bolted Book* (the manifesto as object), Balla's speed-lines (the instrument layer's diagonals). Italian Futurism is the one modern movement born on this sea that was explicitly about *the future* — which is the brief.

## 2. TYPOGRAPHY & PALETTE

### 2.1 Type decision — "the manifesto speaks, the myth whispers"

Three verified OFL families (Google Fonts CSS API, HTTP 200 for these axes — see `design-language.md` §1.1). This is the research's "raid-for-parts" route made whole: the audacious sans + mono of System C ("HELIOS") for the Forum voice, and the display serif of System A ("CALYPSO") for the myth voice only.

| Voice | Family · axes | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **THE MANIFESTO** (Articles, clauses, slogans) | **Archivo** — variable `wdth 62..125`, `wght 100..900`, italic | The only free grotesk with a **125 width** — the Depero/Futurist poster letter, and a 62 condensed cut for dense programme lists from the same file. The width axis is *animatable*: the manifesto physically widens under the sun. |
| **THE MYTH** (marginalia, one italic line per Article) | **Fraunces** — `opsz 9..144`, `wght 100..900`, `SOFT 0..100`, `WONK 0..1`, italic | Old-style Venetian warmth against the machine sans; italic at `wght 300`, `WONK 1` (leaning h/n/m — the hand in the letter). Used **only** in italic, only for script lines. Never roman, never large. |
| **THE INSTRUMENT** (eyebrows, indices, coordinates, chips, form) | **Martian Mono** — `wdth 75..112.5`, `wght 100..800` | The most overtly technical free mono; at `wdth 80` it sits in 9–11 px chips and reads "observatory", not "code". |


Rules:
- Article clauses are set **uppercase, Archivo 700, wdth 110**, and widen to **wdth 125** over the chapter's hold (`font-variation-settings` tween on one element, ≤1.2 s, never on running text). Cover word `SUN` is `wdth 125 wght 800`.
- Lists in the Forum voice: **Archivo wdth 75, wght 500, uppercase, `.02em`**, one item per line, `•` separators replaced by line breaks — a programme reads like a broadside, not a paragraph.
- The myth is *never* uppercase, never bold, never larger than `--t-myth-lg`, and always sits in the **margin column** (left 6–8% or right 7%), never centred.
- `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on every number; stats in Archivo 200 (hairline-thin, huge) — the only light weight on the page, so numbers feel measured rather than shouted.
- `text-wrap: balance` on clauses; `hanging-punctuation: first` on the myth.
- Fallbacks with `size-adjust`: Archivo → `"Archivo Fallback"` (Helvetica Neue/Arial, `size-adjust:98%`), Fraunces → Georgia/Iowan Old Style (`size-adjust:104%`), Martian Mono → ui-monospace/Menlo. `font-display: block` for Archivo (SplitText needs metrics), `swap` for the others.

**Temperature arc across the film** (ground → accent):
`00 limestone + gold ▸ I limestone → press (thunder) ▸ II press + cream stars ▸ III limestone + sea/ramla/stone cut-outs ▸ IV limestone + gold & terra hands ▸ V limestone → three Delaunay suns (gold/flame/ramla) ▸ VI stone floor + ink sectile ▸ VII limestone + ink compass, sea wave-lines ▸ VIII sky-blue cold → gold ▸ IX limestone + olive/silver ▸ X press + cream net → gold hand ▸ XI black ▸ XII limestone + sea + stone planks ▸ XIII Rothko navy → flame → gold → limestone.`

## 3. STORYBOARD — the fourteen states of the Disc

Architecture as in tech-spec §3.4: one sticky `.pin`, one canvas, one master timeline; total ≈ **41,000 px** (≈ 46 vh-units at 900px). Chapters are ranges of progress. Camera is *frontal and long-lensed* (fov 22) so the Disc reads as a poster object; it dollies only in II, X and XII.

**THE DISC — one system, all chapters.** `InstancedMesh` of 12,000 tesserae (mobile 4,000), each a slightly irregular 12 mm quad on a hinge. Attributes: `aRing (1..11)`, `aTheta`, `aRand`, `aHinge (tangent axis)`, `aTargetB/C (vec3)`. Uniforms: `uFlip` (a 128² mask texture — the glyph), `uFlipStagger`, `uExplode`, `uSwarm (0 disc → 1 target B → 2 target C)`, `uFlatten`, `uLight (pointer/lamp)`, `uGold`. Material: physical gold (`metalness 1, roughness .26`, RoomEnvironment PMREM, per-tile ±7° normal tilt — the Ravenna setters) on the front, matte `--press` (or `--olive-silver` in IX) on the back. Cost: one draw call. Every chapter below only changes *uniforms and the mask*.

Copy conventions: `[M]` manifesto voice (Archivo, from the deck, verbatim), `[myth]` marginalia (Fraunces italic, from the script, verbatim), `LABEL` instrument (Martian Mono).

### 00 · COVER — «MEDITERRANEAN SUN»
Emotion: AWE · Ground: limestone · Length 2,400px · Rail: `COVER`

**Frame (film still).** Limestone page, noon. **Midground:** THE DISC, 70% of viewport height, centred slightly right (x 58%), tilted 8° toward the viewer like a wall clock seen from below; its 12,000 gold tiles catch a light that moves with the pointer, so the surface *vibrates* (Alviani). Cut into the gold by flipped ink tiles: the word **SUN** — the Disc is spelling. **Foreground, left column (x 7%, y 30–70%):** the eyebrow `MEDITERRANEAN TOURISM FORUM · 11TH EDITION · MALTA · 25 — 27 NOVEMBER 2026`; beneath it, in Archivo 700 wdth 110, `MEDITERRANEAN` on one line and, below, the three-line clause `STEWARDSHIP / UNITY / NET POSITIVE` stacked with hairlines between. **Background:** nothing but stone and a faint 24 px pencil grid at 6% (Agnes Martin). Bottom third: a single 1 px horizon rule drawn 6%→94% with a crosshair at Malta's longitude (x 62%). Temperature: warm neutral, gold the only saturated thing.

**Scroll 0→1.**
- 0–.15: the Disc completes its preloader assembly (see §7.4) — the last ring locks; `SUN` tiles finish flipping to ink with an 8 ms stagger radiating from the S.
- .15–.45: the three questions arrive one per beat as manifesto chips on the left: `WHAT MUST WE PROTECT?` · `WHAT CAN WE ACHIEVE TOGETHER?` · `WHAT SHOULD TOURISM LEAVE BEHIND?` (Archivo 600 wdth 100, hairline-framed, rules draw left→right, label follows rule). The deck's line `[M] For thousands of years, the Sun has shaped Mediterranean civilisation, architecture, agriculture, food, culture and way of life.` sits under them at `--t-lead`.
- .45–.70: `SUN` letters flip back to gold (the word dissolves into the object); the Disc's rings begin a slow counter-rotation (odd rings +, even rings −, 0.4°/s).
- .70–1: the Disc sinks toward the horizon rule, tilting flat; its gold warms to flame at the rim (`uGold` hue → `--flame-hot`). The marginalia appears for the first time, small, in the right margin: `[myth] The Sun fills the screens. The cattle of Helios.`
- Mouse: ±14 px on the Disc, ±6 px on type; `uLight` follows pointer (the lamp on the vault).

**CTA.** Persistent `REGISTER →` pill (top right). Secondary mono `WATCH MTF10 ↗` (**TBC** link).

**Transition → I.** The Disc touches the horizon; on the stone above the rule, seven ink glyphs (cattle: rectangle + two arcs) tessellate in from the right edge along the rule, one per tick. Rail label → `ARTICLE I`.

### I · ARTICLE I — STEWARDSHIP · «DO NOT TOUCH WHAT BELONGS TO THE SUN.»
Emotion: GRAVITY · Ground: limestone → press · Length 3,200px · S fills gold on exit

**Frame.** A poster: the Disc is now a low flame-gold sun on the horizon (bottom 30%), the seven cattle glyphs stand on the rule in a frieze, each carrying a gold tile (the Sun's brand). Above them, the article clause fills the top half in **Archivo 700 wdth 110, ink on limestone, four lines**: `DO NOT TOUCH / WHAT BELONGS / TO THE SUN.` — the first true manifesto line. Left margin: `[myth] His companions had been warned. / They knew. / And still they did.` Long Boccioni "lines of force" — six 1 px diagonals from the top-right corner toward the cattle — draw in as the clause lands. Temperature: gold-on-stone, then a cut to press-black.

**Scroll 0→1.**
- 0–.30: clause rises line by line (line masks, 60 ms stagger); cattle frieze walks 40 px right in 4 stepped frames (folk stop-motion feel, `steps(4)`); the fifth cow's gold tile flips to *flame* — the taking.
- .30–.42: **THE CRETTO.** Thunder: the ground cuts to press in one frame; the whole frame — Disc, cattle, clause, rules — **cracks along a Voronoi cretto** (post-process `TesseraeEffect` with `uCell 14 → 5`, grout widening to 6 px in `--press`; the DOM type gets the SVG-mask twin so the letters crack too). Tiles shear apart along cluster vectors (`uExplode` 0→1), the Disc breaks into ~40 gold shards that fall below the rule into a sea plane that rises from the bottom edge (Gerstner, storm table). `[myth] Zeus answered for Helios. / Thunder. / The ship breaks apart. / The sailors disappear.`
- .42–.75: over the settling shards, the Forum lands in cream on press: `[M] Tourism brings opportunity — but also responsibility.` · `[M] Visitors, businesses, authorities and communities all have a role in protecting the places we share.` Chip row in `--chip-dark`: `HERITAGE · ENVIRONMENT · COMMUNITIES · CULTURE · DESTINATIONS · SEA`; verb ladder in mono: `PROTECT → RESPECT → PRESERVE → ENHANCE`. `[M] Growth must improve the places people visit — not consume what makes them special.`
- .75–1: the second clause, Archivo 700 wdth 110→125 widening as it holds: `ENJOY THE DESTINATION. / RESPECT THE PLACE.` Sign-off label `PROTECT WHAT MAKES THE MEDITERRANEAN SPECIAL.` The rail's **S** fills gold.

**Transition → II.** Last shards sink; the sea plane darkens to abyss; one shard stays lit at centre — a single gold tile. `[myth] Ulysses alone. / No ship. / No companions. / Only a man… / between sea and sky.` The sunk tiles begin rising as points of light.

### IV · ARTICLE IV — UNITY · «ACHIEVE TOGETHER WHAT WE CANNOT ACHIEVE ALONE.»
Emotion: TENDERNESS · Ground: limestone · Length 2,800px · U fills gold on exit

## 4. FIVE SIGNATURE MOMENTS (nobody has seen these on a conference website)

1. **THE DISC WRITES.** A 12,000-tile gold flip-dot sun that *spells* — `SUN` on the cover, `25 / 26 / 27` on the day columns, `11` on the floor, a nine-digit counter, `XI` at dawn — by flipping tesserae between gold face and ink back, with a lamp that follows your pointer so the surface shimmers like Ravenna under oil light. One InstancedMesh, one mask texture per word. It is the logo, the loader, the progress dial, the hero, the stat display and the footer.
2. **THE CRETTO.** At "Thunder" the *entire page* — WebGL and DOM alike — cracks along a Voronoi cretto and shears apart; the letters of the manifesto break with the sun. A post-process on the frame plus an SVG-mask twin on the type, so it is one crack, not two effects. Burri's Gibellina as a page transition.
3. **FONTANA'S TWO CUTS.** Twice — "the veil tears" (VII→VIII) and "Calypso cuts the net" (X) — a single 2 px slash opens the limestone page like a canvas and reveals depth behind it; the halves part and curl 4° and the camera goes *through the page*. The page is a material, not a surface.

A per-visit seed (shown as a tiny mono string in the instrument layer) generates a unique gold tessera — ring, angle, tilt, glint — as an SVG card with the dates and `I WAS THERE · MTF11`, shareable as an OG-sized image; every registrant's tile is added to a live "1,600" mosaic on the Register chapter (count from the CMS, **TBC**). Honourable: **the dead zone** — 300 px of scroll in Article XI where nothing responds after "I am Homer." A conference site that dares to hold.

## 8. MOBILE STRATEGY (portrait ≤ 4/5 aspect)

## 9. BUILD-RISK LIST (with mitigations)

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Disc's flip mask looks like a pixel display, not mosaic** (reads "LED sign") | High | Tiles are jittered quads with 3 px grout and ±7° per-tile tilt; flips stagger by `aRand` (never row-scan); mask edges are dithered to the ring geometry, not a square grid; gold PBR with PMREM so faces read as metal. Review at `?chapter=cover&p=.1` before anything else is built. |
| 2 | **12,000 hinged instances + physical gold material too slow on iGPU/mobile** | Medium | One draw call; `MeshPhysicalMaterial` only on desktop `high`, a custom cheap gold `ShaderMaterial` (fresnel + env lookup) on `mid/low`; 4,000 tiles on mobile; DPR governor (tech-spec §8.2); budget ≤ 6 ms scene render. |
| 3 | **The Cretto on the DOM** (SVG mask twin on live text) mis-registers with the GL crack or tanks paint performance | Medium | Snapshot approach: at .30 the type block is replaced by a `<canvas>` copy (html→canvas of *our own* text is trivial: same font, same layout, drawn by `Text.ts`) for the 0.6 s of shatter; real DOM returns at .42. Fallback: DOM simply fades under the GL crack. |
| 4 | **Archivo width-axis tweens cause reflow and jank** | Medium | Tween `font-variation-settings` only on a single absolutely-positioned clause with fixed box (`width` set from the wdth-125 measurement so no reflow), ≤ 1.2 s, `will-change: contents` off; never on lists or body. |
| 5 | **Light ground + grain + gold** looks "warm template" instead of "modernist" | Medium | Enforce the poster rule: per frame one giant primitive, one rule, one mono label; hard-edged fills, no gradients on shapes (Rothko fields only in VIII/XIII); `--grain .05` max; Agnes Martin grid at 6% only. Kill it if a screenshot reads as "Etsy". |
| 6 | **Fontana cut** (`#tear` displacement + page halves parting) is heavy on Safari | Medium | The "page" is a GL quad with the current frame as texture (one extra render target, allowed by the ≤ 3 passes budget) — the halves move in GL, not CSS; DOM type fades 60 ms before the cut. |
| 7 | **Myth marginalia is too quiet — the client wanted "story"** | Medium | The four myth headlines (I, VII, VIII, X) are promoted to `--t-myth-lg`; the Disc carries the narrative *physically* (cattle → cretto → stars → hand); Storyteller lines are never cut, only sized. If the client wants more, `--t-myth` scales up one step with no layout change. |
| 8 | **Contrast:** gold on limestone in CTAs/labels | High if unpoliced | Lint rule: `--gold*` may only be applied to backgrounds, borders, SVG fills and GL; text colour tokens are `--ink / --ink-soft / --terra / --cream / --gold-leaf(on press only)`. |
| 9 | **TBC content** (venue, registration URLs, speakers, gala tickets, partners, hotel) blocks "finished" chapters | Certain | Every TBC has a designed *complete* state: `VENUE TO BE ANNOUNCED` chip, `TBA` rims, `BY INVITATION (TBC)` meta; all from `src/data/*.json` so a one-line change ships. |
| 10 | **Speaker photo quality** (WordPress uploads of varying size/colour) | High | Duotone cut-out arches equalise everything; minimum 480 px; missing image → an ink arch with initials in Archivo 200. Image rights **TBC**. |
| 11 | **Disk space** (2.2 GB free at time of writing; tech-spec found 136 MB) | Medium | ≥ 2 GB is now free — install immediately; no Playwright in `package.json`; fonts subset to ~180 KB. |
| 12 | **Timeline: 81 days; one build-day for the agents** | Certain | Build order: (1) core + Disc + Cover + Colophon (the loop), (2) Cretto + I, (3) VIII (the title line), (4) X (hand), (5) V/VI/VII (the Forum's density), (6) the rest.
