## Round 3 — the sun's depth, reading breaks, and the jank (2026-09-05 night)
Client: "the sun is still rising in the foreground and in front of the sea"; "sunrise and set has to happen at the horizon and only when it comes above the sea it must be visible"; "add some break mechanism at least for all the sentences and words that are coming in"; "the scroll is too laggy, sometimes it skips frames and sometimes it is too laggy".

- **The sun is now a celestial body.** A chapter declares where the sun should *appear*; the layer treats that as a direction and puts the disc 140 units out along the same ray, scaling its radius by the same factor. Screen position and apparent size are unchanged, but it is now beyond every object, so the sea cuts it at the horizon and it is only visible above the water — the ball floating in front of the waves is gone (Ch 02's ember and Ch 06's daylight verified).
- **The breath.** `createFilm` now maps scroll to timeline time through the chapter's own beats: the moment a beat animates runs at its natural speed, and the still moment after it is given 2.2× the scroll, so a sentence lands and holds while you read. The pauses are paid for by growing the section (capped at 1.6×), never by speeding the beats up — that would have read as skipping. The Stage reads the same map, so the world and the copy stay on one clock and moments like the Shatter still coincide. Page 69k → 78k px.
- **The jank — measured, not guessed.** `scripts/perf.mjs` (real wheel input) and an in-page probe showed 19–30% of frames over 32 ms. Instrumenting the frame proved our own code was not the cost: stage.frame p95 0.2 ms, world.update 0.5 ms, composer.render 0.5 ms. The browser was styling, laying out and painting all fourteen chapters every frame. `content-visibility: auto` with `contain-intrinsic-size` from `--film-len` lets it skip the thirteen off screen: **jank 18.8% → 5%, p95 91 ms → 32 ms, p99 175 ms → 50 ms**. Also: off-screen chapters no longer run their idle CSS animations (59 were running at once), the live CSS custom properties are quantised and only written when they change (each write invalidated the whole document's styles), the veil and rail read those values from the inline style instead of forcing a style flush, the mood blend no longer allocates two Mood objects per frame, and the scrub came down to .85 so the scroll feels answered rather than delayed.

## Round 6 — "every sentence needs four scrolls" (2026-09-06)
Client: "the scroll has become way too long, 17 minutes, every word is loading after like 4 scrolls — the first screen especially."
The hold was gating every arrival and throwing away any swipe made before the two seconds were up. Simple fix, four parts:
- a swipe made against a closed gate is remembered and released the moment the time is up (to the next sentence, whole);
- a swipe that reaches into a sentence's arrival completes it (no half-faded lines, no second swipe to finish one);
- only text that is about to LEAVE the screen is held — a headline stays while the story lands under it, the older lines of a stack stay faint — so a reader moves at their own pace and only a flick that would erase something unread is stopped; a stanza landed by one flick owes its lines' time in sequence, capped at 4 s;

## Round 7 — the wheel means "next" (2026-09-06)

Each star is now its own. 278 stops across the film, ~20 a chapter.
- **The gates are gone** with the scrub: nothing to meter, nothing to hold, no reading cue (src/ui/holdcue.* deleted). The player still rests on every sentence for its reading time.
